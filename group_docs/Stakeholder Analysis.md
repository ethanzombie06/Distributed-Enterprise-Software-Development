# Stakeholder Analysis Introduction

This stakeholder analysis introduces the main people and organisations
affected by **Use Case 1: Producer Product Listing and Inventory Management**.
The use case focuses on producers adding products to the marketplace,
validating product and certification information, and managing stock and
seasonal availability.

The most important stakeholder is the **registered producer**, who needs a
clear process for creating listings, correcting invalid information, and
updating product quantities. **Customers** rely on the marketplace to show
accurate product, allergen, origin, certification, price, and availability
information.

Other important stakeholders include the **marketplace administrator**, who
manages errors and exceptional cases; the **certification verification
service**, which checks organic claims; and the **marketplace development
team**, which implements and tests the process. The **marketplace owner** and
relevant **food-safety or regulatory bodies** are also involved because they
set business, compliance, and trust requirements.

Understanding these stakeholders will help us decide what information
the system must collect, how validation and certification failures should be
handled, and when a product should be made available or unavailable to
customers. Further stakeholder analysis will be completed as the requirements
and business rules become clearer.

## Stakeholders and roles

| Stakeholder                         | Role                                                                                                 | Importance |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Registered producer                 | Creates product listings, provides product information, and updates stock and seasonal availability. | High       |
| Customer                            | Browses products and relies on accurate product, price, certification, and availability information. | High       |
| Marketplace administrator           | Manages listings, handles validation or certification issues, and supports users.                    | High       |
| Certification verification service  | Checks whether an organic certification claim is valid.                                              | Medium     |
| Marketplace development team        | Designs, implements, and tests the listing and inventory process.                                    | High       |
| Marketplace owner or sponsor        | Defines business priorities, policies, and success criteria.                                         | High       |
| Food-safety or regulatory authority | Provides compliance expectations for product, allergen, origin, and certification information.       | Medium     |
