
### Assessment Task 1 Clarifications: Case Study and Use Case Selection

For Task 1, each group is expected to **select ONE of the three use cases** provided on Blackboard.

- **Case Study:** Provides the overall context and detailed scenario for the project.
- **Three Use Cases:** Provide a breakdown of the specific requirements within the case study.

Each group only needs to **select and work on ONE use case**, not all three.

### Stand-up Arrangements and Marking
Please be reminded of the following:

- There are **9 stand-ups in total** during Term 1.
- Stand-ups are **assessed individually**, not as a group.
- Your **6 highest stand-up scores** out of the 9 will be used to calculate your individual stand-up mark.
- Therefore, it is perfectly fine to miss one stand-up if you are unable to attend, as **only your six highest scores will count.**
- **All stand-ups must take place in person** during your allocated practical sessions. _Online or remote stand-ups will not be accepted unless there are approved extenuating circumstances from the university (e.g., Student Support Advisor team)._
